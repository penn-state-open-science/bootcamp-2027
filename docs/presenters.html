Ivan Jacob Agaloos Pesigan (Jek)

Ivan Jacob Agaloos Pesigan (Jek), Ph.D. is an Assistant Research Professor in the Quantitative Developmental Systems Methodology Core (QuantDev) in the Social Science Research Institute at Penn State.

Jek specializes in quantitative methodology, statistical modeling, and computational research, with a focus on developing statistical methods and software for analyzing longitudinal data. An advocate for open science and computational reproducibility, his interests extend to research software development, containerization, and high-performance computing. His work bridges statistical methodology and reproducible computational practices to advance transparent, accessible, and reliable scientific research.
